Summary

Gideon compared the three documents field by field. Three discrepancies were found. Each document reads correctly on its own — none is individually malformed — but they do not agree with each other. This is the pattern Gideon is built to catch: consistency across a document set, not just correctness within one document.

Discrepancies

High

1. Shipper / seller name does not match across documents

DocumentFieldValue
Commercial InvoiceSellerMeridian Commodities Pte Ltd
Packing ListSellerMeridian Commodities Pte Ltd
Bill of LadingShipperMeridian Commodity Trading Pte Ltd

The entity named as shipper on the Bill of Lading is not the same legal name as the seller on the Invoice and Packing List. This could be a related entity, a clerical error, or a sign the B/L was issued by a different party than the one contracted. Confirm which entity holds the contract and verify both names against the relevant company registry.

High

2. Quantity mismatch between Invoice and Packing List

DocumentQuantity
Commercial Invoice500.000 MT
Packing List (net)480.000 MT
Bill of Lading500.000 MT

The Packing List understates the cargo by 20 metric tons (4%) against both the Invoice and the Bill of Lading. Two of three documents agree at 500.000 MT, which points to the Packing List as the likely source of the error — but that should be confirmed with the seller, not assumed.

Medium

3. Shipment date conflicts with Bill of Lading on-board date

DocumentFieldValue
Commercial InvoiceShipment Date11 September 2026
Bill of LadingShipped on Board09 September 2026

The Invoice states the goods shipped two days after the Bill of Lading was already issued confirming the goods were on board. A B/L cannot be issued before loading occurs, so one of these two dates is wrong. Confirm the actual loading date with the carrier or port agent.

What this report does not do

Gideon flags what does not reconcile and tells you exactly where to look. It does not verify entities against a company registry, confirm certificate authenticity with an issuing body, or replace a human sign-off. Items marked High above should be independently checked before funds move.
